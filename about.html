About Us

The guiding principles, vision, and mission of Cordova Public College.

Our Vision

"A community-based college that shall offer quality education at a low tuition fee."

Our Mission

  • To offer affordable and quality education, primarily but not exclusively, to the Cordovanhons with the end in view that education shall be within everyone's reach.
  • To provide tertiary education, technical, vocational and other practical courses.

Core Values

Patriotism Respect Accessibility Integrity Spirituality Excellence Service

Goals

The college undertakes to carry the different goals:

01

To foster, sustain and support the knowledge proficiency, and professional levels essential to employment;

02

To develop the capacity to grow into fully qualified professional leaders;

03

To cultivate the spirit of nationalism enhancing individual and social awareness of society and community; and

04

To inculcate a strong sense of commitment and dedication to any profession guided by faith and strong moral fervor.

Philosophy

The college is established in order to:

01

Inculcate desirable values, virtues, love and service for fellowmen for spiritual and moral upliftment, intellectual integrity, social and individual fulfillment;

02

Develop intellectually motivated individuals demonstrating scientific training through related academic activities; research and evaluation that will contribute to the local and national development;

03

Train for democratic leadership characterized by commitment to the vision-mission of the institution;

04

Contribute to the socio-economic, technological, scientific, and cultural growth of man in a society; and

05

Produce self-reliant citizens imbued with love of his/her community and country.
